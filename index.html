Projections

💰 Liquid Cash Mode (Recommended): Shows what you would have if you sold everything today.
  • Deducts Selling Costs (Estate Agent fees, etc).
  • Deducts Exit Taxes (Capital Gains Tax, Corporation Tax).
  • Why use this? It's the only fair way to compare "Cash in the Bank" (Rent) vs "Equity in a House" (Buy).
📄 Gross Equity Mode: Shows the "Paper Value" of your assets.
  • Ignores selling fees and taxes.
  • Useful if you never plan to sell the property.
  • Warning: Makes Property look wealthier than it actually is compared to Renting.

"Dead Money" is unrecoverable cost: Rent, Mortgage Interest, Maintenance, Taxes, and Buying Fees.
