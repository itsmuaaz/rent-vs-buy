A. Rent & Invest

You continue renting. All surplus cash (savings + deposit money) is invested in the Stock Market (ISA & GIA).

B. Buy Solo

You buy a home in your name and live alone. You build equity but pay mortgage interest and maintenance costs.

C. Buy + Lodger

You buy a home and rent out a spare room. The income is Tax-Free (up to £7,500/yr) and offsets your mortgage.

D. Company BTL

You rent personally, but use your capital to start a Ltd Company and buy a Buy-to-Let property (Tax efficient for high earners).
