# Product Definition

## Vision
To provide UK residents and investors with a professional-grade financial simulation tool that offers accurate, 40-year wealth projections. The goal is to move beyond simple mortgage calculators and empower users to make data-driven decisions between renting, buying a home, and property investing (Buy-to-Let), all while navigating complex UK tax rules.

## Target Audience
- **UK Residents:** Individuals and families evaluating the long-term financial impact of renting versus buying a home.
- **Property Investors:** Users analyzing Buy-to-Let returns, comparing personal ownership versus Limited Company structures.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Long-Term Perspective:** Simulates a 40-year financial lifespan rather than just monthly payments.
- **Tax Accuracy:** Incorporates real-world UK tax logic (Stamp Duty, Section 24, Capital Gains, Corporation Tax).
- **Strategic Comparison:** Enables side-by-side comparison of multiple financial paths (e.g., Rent & Invest vs. Buy vs. BTL).
- **Transparency:** "Audit Mode" provides step-by-step proofs for every calculation, ensuring trust in the numbers.

## Key Features
- **Multi-Strategy Simulation:** Compare Rent & Invest, Buy Home, Buy + Lodger, BTL (Personal), BTL (Ltd), and Hybrids.
- **Advanced Tax Engine:**
    - **Stamp Duty (SDLT):** Supports standard rates, First Time Buyer relief, and Additional Dwelling Supplement.
    - **Capital Gains Tax (CGT):** Models allowances and rate bands for property and shares.
    - **Rent a Room Scheme:** Handles tax-free lodger income.
    - **Corporation Tax:** For Limited Company BTL scenarios.
- **True Valuation Modes:** Toggles between Gross Equity (Paper Wealth) and Liquid Cash (Post-tax/fee realization).
- **Economic Adjustments:**
    - **Inflation Adjustment:** "Real Terms" toggle to view future wealth in today's purchasing power.
    - **Stress Testing:** Simulates market crashes (Stock Market -30% or Property Market -15%) to test strategy resilience.
    - **Sensitivity Matrix:** Heatmap visualization comparing Rent vs. Buy outcomes across varying interest rates and property growth scenarios.
- **Detailed Analytics:**
    - **Dead Money Analysis:** Visualizes unrecoverable costs (Interest, Tax, Maintenance, Fees) vs. Equity growth.
    - **Time Travel Inspector:** Interactive scrubber to inspect financial state at any year in the simulation.
- **Smart Inputs & Tools:**
    - **Budget Lock:** Automatically balances Rent and Savings to maintain a fixed Total Monthly Budget.
    - **Dual-Mode Sync:** Seamlessly switch between Percentage and Amount inputs for Deposits and Yields.
    - **Smart Estimates:** Auto-calculates buying costs based on property price.
